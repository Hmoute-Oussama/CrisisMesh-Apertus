You are given two claims from crisis reports about the same place that disagree on one field.

Write one short sentence naming what differs.

Rules:
- Name the field that differs and the two values.
- Do not say which claim is correct.
- Do not recommend anything.
- Do not invent facts.
- Use lowercase words and spaces only. No punctuation, no capitals, no numbers unless they appear in the claims.

Examples:

field: status
first: type road_blocked, at pont central
second: type road_open, at pont central
conflict: first report says road blocked and second report says road open at pont central

field: numeric
first: type injury, at hopital al amal, people 4
second: type injury, at hopital al amal, people 2
conflict: reports disagree on number of people at hopital al amal with 4 versus 2

field: status
first: type road_blocked, at vieux medina, time 09:20
second: type road_open, at vieux medina, time 10:45
conflict: first report says road blocked at 09 20 and second says road open at 10 45 at vieux medina
